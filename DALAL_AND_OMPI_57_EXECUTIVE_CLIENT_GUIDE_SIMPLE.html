OMPI Management Suite Overview • The 4 Business Engines to be Built Proposed Operations Suite
What the OMPI Management System Will Do for Your Company

While Dalal Machine Tools will act as your public digital showroom that attracts factory owners, OMPI India will operate as your internal business management system. It will organize everything that happens after an inquiry arrives: quoting prices, sizing clutches, placing orders with Italy, tracking sea shipments, managing godown stock, and collecting customer payments.

Engine 1: Sales & Customer Quotes (10 Tools)

Will store client GST records, size the right clutch, generate official PDF proposals in 60 seconds, and track deals from new inquiry to advance payment.

Engine 2: Italy Purchase & Import Cost (7 Tools)

Will create Euro purchase orders for OMPI Italy, calculate total landed customs duty in Rupees, and track sea containers from Genoa to Nhava Sheva.

Engine 3: Godown Stock & Spare Parts (8 Tools)

Will show live godown stock in Pune and Chakan, track high-wear brake friction pads, record serial numbers, and create truck delivery passes.

Engine 4: Bills, GST Taxes, Money & Safety (8 Tools)

Will generate official GST bills, export GSTR-1 for your accountant in 1 click, track customer balance payments, and save automatic daily backups.

Proposed Phased Delivery Roadmap (Upon Project Commencement)

Phase 1 Delivery Scope: The 24 public website pages, staff inquiry inbox, 60-second PDF proposal generator, and stock tracking tools will be engineered and launched first.
Phase 2 Delivery Scope: In-depth ERP tools (Euro purchase orders, multi-level parts breakdown, laser serial tracking, and GSTR-1 export) will be delivered across 7 monthly sprints as agreed in the Master SOW contract.

Data Safety Standard to be Built: All company inquiries, customer phone numbers, supplier costs, and pricing records will be password-protected. Staff members will only see what their job requires, and complete backups will be automatically saved every night.
