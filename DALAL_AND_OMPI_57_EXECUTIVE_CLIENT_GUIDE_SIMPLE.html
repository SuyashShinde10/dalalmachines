How the timeline works

  • Start date. The clock starts when the MSA is signed, the first payment is received, and the Phase 1 content (photos, machine details, videos) is handed over. If your content or feedback is late, the dates move by the same number of days.
  • Testing and acceptance. At the end of each phase you try the system (this is called user acceptance testing, or UAT) and confirm it works as described here. Please give feedback within 7 days. If we hear nothing within 7 days, the phase is treated as accepted, unless you have reported a problem in writing.
  • 30-day warranty. From acceptance of each phase, we fix any defect (something not working as this document describes) at no extra charge for 30 days. New features or changes are handled as change requests (section 5).

3. What Is and Is Not Included

Included: design, development, testing, deployment and go-live of the 57 units described in sections 6 and 7. All systems are web-based and work on phone, tablet and computer browsers.

Not included

  • Hardware: CCTV cameras, recorders, internet or fibre line and fixed IP at the Pune yard, servers, computers, barcode scanners and label printers, office Wi-Fi.
  • Fees of outside services (see section 4).
  • Content creation: photography, video shooting, and writing legal agreements or tax advice.
  • Typing in your old records. We provide import templates and load the spreadsheets you supply.
  • Separate Android or iPhone apps.
  • Filing GST returns or replacing your accounting software. The ERP prepares data for your CA; it does not file anything on the government portal.
  • Features not described in this document, and support after the 30-day warranty. Ongoing support is available as an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC).

5. Key Terms in Plain Words

The MSA is the governing agreement. This section is a summary.

  • One quotation tool, two companies. Phase 1 builds one quotation engine used by the website download (Unit 11), the enquiry desk (Unit 24) and the ERP (Module 04). Each company's quotations carry its own letterhead, numbering and bank details.
  • Security and backups. Every staff member has their own login. Roles decide what each person can see, for example purchase prices only for management. Data is stored in an encrypted database with automatic daily backups. Our target is to keep 30 days of backups; the exact period depends on the hosting plan you choose. A restore test is done before each system goes live.
  • Hosting. Cloud hosting is the default. If you prefer an office server for the ERP, we will confirm that in the MSA.
  • Estimates only. The tonnage assistant (Unit 15), the FAQ and High Seas guides (Units 10, 16) and the price and cost calculators (Modules 03, 15) give estimates and general information. They are not engineering, legal or tax advice. Final decisions rest with your engineers and CA.
  • Change requests. Anything not described here is estimated and agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Ownership. Custom code and designs made for you transfer to the relevant company after full payment, as set out in the MSA. Third-party and open-source components stay under their own licences, and the consultant's general-purpose tools that are not specific to your business remain the consultant's.
  • Confidentiality. This document and your business data are kept confidential.

8. How the Two Systems Work Together

  • Customer enquires. A plant head finds a press on the website and taps the WhatsApp button, or fills a form. (Units 1, 5, 9)
  • Technical sizing. Your sales engineer looks up the matching OMPI clutch. (Modules 02, 06)
  • Quotation sent. An official quotation with photos, specifications and price is produced in moments. (Unit 11, Module 04)
  • Stock reserved. Once terms are agreed, the machine is marked Reserved so nobody else can commit it. (Unit 23, Module 07)
  • Workshop and delivery. Overhaul, power trial, inspection, transport and site handover are recorded. (Modules 17 to 20)
  • Billing and records. GST invoice, payment tracking and a monthly summary for your CA. (Modules 21 to 25)
