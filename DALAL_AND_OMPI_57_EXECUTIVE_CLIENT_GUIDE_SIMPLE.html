OMPI Operations Suite Overview • The 4 Business Engines Internal Company ERP System
How OMPI Operations Management Works in Daily Practice

While Dalal Machine Tools operates as the public digital showroom that brings in inquiries from factory owners, OMPI India operates the internal management system. It organizes everything that happens after an inquiry arrives: quoting prices, sizing clutches, placing orders with Italy, tracking sea shipments, managing godown inventory, and collecting customer payments.

Engine 1: Sales & Customer Quotes (10 Tools)

Stores client GST records, sizes the right clutch, generates official PDF proposals in 60 seconds, and tracks deals from new inquiry to advance payment.

Engine 2: Italy Purchase & Import Cost (7 Tools)

Creates Euro purchase orders for OMPI Italy, calculates total landed customs duty in Rupees, and tracks sea containers from Genoa to Nhava Sheva.

Engine 3: Godown Stock & Spare Parts (8 Tools)

Shows live godown stock in Pune and Chakan, tracks high-wear brake friction pads, records serial numbers, and creates truck delivery passes.

Engine 4: Bills, GST Taxes, Money & Safety (8 Tools)

Generates official GST bills, exports GSTR-1 for your accountant in 1 click, tracks customer balance payments, and saves automatic daily backups.

Current Implementation Status: What is Live vs. Phased Rollout

10 Core Tools Are Already Live: The 60-second PDF quotation engine, AI clutch matching, inquiry pipeline tracking, WhatsApp quote sender, machine inventory stock tracking, staff access security, and nightly backups are already functioning in the system.
Deep ERP Tools (Phase 2): Specialized tools like Euro purchase orders, multi-level parts breakdown, laser serial tracking, and GSTR-1 tax export are fully architected in this document and scheduled for rollout across 7 monthly sprints as agreed in the Master SOW contract.

Data Safety Commitment: All company inquiries, customer phone numbers, supplier costs, and pricing records are strictly protected. Staff members only see what their job requires, and complete backups are automatically saved every night.
