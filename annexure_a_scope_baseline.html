Part 1: Dalal Machine Tools Digital Web Platform & Sales Engine

The Dalal Platform is engineered as a high-performance corporate catalog and buyer lead-generation engine designed to showcase 50,000 sq. ft. of heavy industrial machinery stock to national manufacturing enterprises:

1.1 24-Page Public Corporate Platform Public Web
  • Full 24-Page Architecture: Responsive public portal including Homepage, About the Agency, Pune Stockyard Showcase (50,000 sq. ft.), 26 Industrial Category Landing Pages, Machine Detail Template, High Seas Sales Calculator, Power Trial Inspection Booking, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use.
  • Up to 60 Machinery Catalog Ceiling: Structured onboarding of up to 60 distinct machinery stock items with technical parameters, high-res galleries, dimensions, tonnage, and condition status.
  • Pune Stockyard Showcase: Dedicated visual showcase highlighting warehouse power infrastructure, live trial capabilities, crane capacities, and inspection scheduling.
  • High Seas Sales Calculator: Interactive commercial import landing cost and customs duty savings estimator for corporate machinery buyers.
1.2 Sales Conversion & Technical Discovery Conversion Engine
  • 1-Click Branded PDF Spec Sheets: Automated generator producing standardized A4 machinery spec sheets on official Dalal letterhead for buyer technical committees.
  • Direct WhatsApp RFQ Routing: Instant inquiry dispatch to Director's WhatsApp with machine SKU, tonnage, buyer name, and commercial requirements pre-filled.
  • 1-Click Excel/CSV Leads Exporter: Instant RFC-4180 compliant offline downloads with UTF-8 BOM encoding for direct Microsoft Excel compatibility.
  • Google SEO & Rich Schema.org Markup: Clean semantic HTML5, dynamic XML sitemaps (83 canonical URLs), robots.txt crawler policies, and JSON-LD Product schema.
  • AI Search (GEO) Feeds: Live /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt feeds formatted for AI search scrapers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot).
  • Google Telemetry: Google Tag Manager (GTM-DMTA2026), GA4 custom event tracking, and Google Search Console DNS ownership verification.
1.3 Staff Admin CMS & Super-Admin Security Portals Internal Control
  • Staff Admin CMS Portal: Inventory CRUD management, machine availability toggles (In Stock, Under Inspection, Sold), price updates, category tagging, and customer inquiry review dashboard.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular separation between Staff Editors, Sales Executives, and Super-Administrators.
  • Super-Admin Security Center: Zero-trust cloaking returning HTTP 404 to unauthorized users, cryptographic signed gate pass cookies (dmta_gate_pass), and developer audit console.
  • Cryptographic Tamper-Evident Audit Log: SHA-256 hashed ledger tracking staff logins, inventory modifications, and inquiry dispatches.

Part 3: OMPI India Central ERP System (33 Documented Modules)

The OMPI Central ERP deploys on a dedicated Host PC within the Pune office (http://192.168.x.x:3000), providing offline-first security and multi-user access across 4 operational engines comprising exactly 33 modules:

Group 1: Sales & Customer Pipeline (10 Modules)
  • 1. Customer Master: Billing/shipping branches, GSTIN, PAN, credit limits.
  • 2. Customer 360° Profile: Single-pane audit of orders, quotes, and dues.
  • 3. Enquiry Pipeline: Status stages with mandatory lost-deal forensics.
  • 4. Costing Sheets: Landed cost, gross margin % & profit analytics.
  • 5. Quotation Engine: Immutable revisions (V1, V2) with manager sign-off.
  • 6. Customer PO (CPO): PO validation, delivery terms & variance check.
  • 7. Sales Order (SO): Confirmed orders linked directly to live stock.
  • 8. Proforma Invoice (PI): Configurable milestone splits (e.g. 30/50/20).
  • 9. Communication Log: Time-stamped staff follow-up history.
  • 10. Approval Workflow: Manager authorization for custom quotes.
Group 2: Purchase & Italy Landed Cost (7 Modules)
  • 11. Supplier Master: OMPI Italy & domestic tooling vendor profiles.
  • 12. Supplier RFQs: Price & lead-time requests directly to Italy.
  • 13. Supplier Quotations: Multi-vendor comparison & currency tracking.
  • 14. Supplier PO (SPO): Advance, BL milestone & dispatch terms in EUR.
  • 15. Multi-Currency FX Engine: EUR to INR rates via manual entry or online fetch.
  • 16. Customs & Landed Cost Calculator: Landed unit cost (Freight + Port + Duty).
  • 17. Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Physical tally against Italian packing list.
Group 3: Spares Inventory & Retrofits (8 Modules)
  • 18. SKU Spares Inventory: Clutch plates, discs, seals, and valves.
  • 19. Godown Bin Mapping: Rack/bin coordinate mapping in Pune warehouse.
  • 20. Stock Transfers: Inter-warehouse transfers between locations.
  • 21. Delivery Challans (DC): Full and partial dispatch challans.
  • 22. Security Gate Passes: Returnable & non-returnable security gate passes.
  • 23. Digital Job Work Cards: On-site technician press clutch retrofits.
  • 24. Quality Control (QC): Defect logging, tolerance and rework tracking.
  • 25. RMA & Warranty Claims: Damaged unit returns direct to Italy.
Group 4: Finance, Reporting & Admin (8 Modules)
  • 26. GST Sales Invoicing: Automated CGST/SGST/IGST (HSN 8483).
  • 27. Credit & Debit Notes: Rate variance adjustments & returns.
  • 28. Accounts Receivable: Payment receipts & customer ageing tracker.
  • 29. Profitability Analytics: Gross profit per transaction & part.
  • 30. Universal Search: Instant search across serials, POs, items.
  • 31. Automated PDF Engine: 1-Click branded invoice & quote dispatch.
  • 32. Role-Based Access (RBAC): Granular permissions by staff role.
  • 33. Disaster Recovery: Automated 3-tier rolling database backups.

Part 4: Explicit Scope Exclusions & Change Request Governance

To maintain delivery discipline and avoid timeline dilution, the following deliverables are explicitly excluded from this turnkey scope:

Excluded Domain Explicit Boundary Definition Commercial Policy
Hardware & LAN Cabling Server PC hardware, laptops, UPS units, Ethernet switches, Wi-Fi routers, and physical cabling. Client Procured
Backlog Data Entry Manual typing of historical paper invoices, physical stock ledger books, or legacy records. Client Responsibility
Photography & Videography Professional photo shoots, video tours, or drone footage of stockyard machinery at Pune yard. Client Provided
Corrupt Legacy Migration Cleansing or programmatic ETL conversion from corrupt, unstructured historical Excel sheets. Quoted Separately
Native Mobile App Packages Native Apple App Store (.ipa) and Google Play Store (.apk) app packaging (responsive PWA included). Phase 2 Roadmap
Govt e-Invoicing (IRN) API Automated real-time IRN/QR generation via government GSP portal (requires client GSP account). Quoted Separately
External Accounting Connectors Direct live bidirectional REST APIs connecting to third-party suites (e.g. Tally Prime API, SAP). Quoted Separately
Change Request (CR) Protocol: Any modification to the documented 33 modules, additions beyond 60 catalog items, or requests for excluded capabilities shall be processed through a formal Change Request (CR) form documenting commercial impact and timeline adjustment, commencing solely upon mutual written approval.
