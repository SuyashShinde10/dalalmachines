ERP-MOD-01 Client Records & GST Credit Master ○  TO BE BUILT: SYSTEM 2 Engine 1: Sales & Customer Pipeline
Responsible Staff Role: Sales Executives, Accounts Manager
WHAT THIS MANAGEMENT TOOL WILL DO ONCE BUILT
Will maintain a central profile for every client factory:
verified 15−digit GSTIN, multiple delivery godown
addresses, authorized contact persons, and approved
credit limits.
BUSINESS VALUE & COMMERCIAL RISK IT PREVENTS
Stops costly billing errors, prevents dispatching goods to
companies with overdue payments, and ensures tax
invoices match GST portal records.
Key Management Feature: Automated GST number validation and company ledger outstanding summary.

to search through paper product catalogs.
