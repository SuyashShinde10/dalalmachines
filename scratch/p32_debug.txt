1. LAN-FIRST RESILIENCE & DEPLOYMENT TOPOLOGY
The dual-system architecture utilizes a hybrid edge
deployment. The public commercial portal is distributed via
global CDN edge nodes with aggressive client-side caching.
The OMPI industrial ERP executes primarily on a dedicated
local area network (LAN) edge server situated inside the Pune
stockyard and workshop, guaranteeing sub-5ms internal
transaction response times and 100% operational autonomy
during external internet disruptions.
2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC SECURITY & IMMUTABLE AUDIT
TRAIL
Zero-Trust Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforces strict
least-privilege boundaries across 5 operational tiers: Sales,
Technical Engineering, Warehouse Operations, Statutory
Finance, and Executive Leadership. Every pricing
modification, quotation discount override, credit limit
change, and invoice status update generates an append-
only transaction record sealed with SHA-256 cryptographic
hashes for forensic auditability.
3. STATUTORY TAX & CUSTOMS COMPLIANCE
Deep harmonization with Indian Customs (CBIC) and GST
statutory frameworks. System enforces automated 8−digit
HSN mapping (8483 for mechanical clutch-brakes and
transmission components, 6813 for friction materials, 8462
for machine tool presses). Generates compliant B2B tax
invoices with CGST/SGST/IGST breakdowns, Rule 55 delivery
challans, and one-click JSON exports for direct GSTR-1 portal
filing.
4. DISASTER RECOVERY & REDUNDANT SNAPSHOTS
Automated zero-downtime database snapshots execute
every 24 hours to an isolated Network Attached Storage
(NAS) array. Nightly encrypted archives are transmitted to a
secondary off-site location. Disaster recovery protocols are
tested quarterly, providing a Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
of <24 hours and a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of <15
minutes.
