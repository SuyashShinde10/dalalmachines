Comprehensive Breakdown of OMPI ERP Modules

The OMPI ERP system is structured across 4 functional engines covering all aspects of sales, international procurement from OMPI Italy, warehouse stock keeping, field servicing, and GST billing:

1. Sales & Customer Pipeline (10 Modules)
  • Customer Master: Billing/shipping branches, GSTIN, PAN.
  • Customer 360° View: Single-pane audit of all client documents.
  • Enquiry Pipeline: Status stages with mandatory lost analysis.
  • Costing Sheet: Landed cost, gross margin % & profit analytics.
  • Quotation Engine: Immutable revisions (V1, V2) with approvals.
  • Customer PO (CPO): PO validation, delivery terms & variance check.
  • Sales Order (SO): Confirmed orders linked to sourcing desk.
  • Proforma Invoice (PI): Configurable milestone splits (e.g. 30/50/20).
  • Communication Log: Time-stamped staff follow-up history.
  • Approval Workflow: Manager authorization for custom quotes.
2. Purchase & Italy Landed Cost (7 Modules)
  • Supplier Master: OMPI Italy & domestic tooling vendor profiles.
  • Supplier Enquiries & RFQs: Price & lead-time requests to Italy.
  • Supplier Quotations: Multi-vendor comparison & exchange tracking.
  • Supplier PO (SPO): Advance, BL milestone & dispatch terms.
  • Multi-Currency FX Engine: Live EUR to INR conversion.
  • Customs & Duty Calculator: Landed unit cost (Freight + Port).
  • Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Physical tally against customs packing list.
3. Warehouse, Spares & Retrofits (8 Modules)
  • SKU Spares Inventory: Clutch plates, friction discs, seals, valves.
  • Bin Location Engine: Rack/bin coordinate mapping in Pune godown.
  • Stock Transfer: Inter-warehouse transfers between branches.
  • Delivery Challans (DC): Full and partial dispatch challans.
  • Gate Passes: Returnable & non-returnable security gate passes.
  • Digital Job Work Cards: On-site technician press clutch retrofits.
  • Quality Control (QC): Defect logging, tolerance and rework tracking.
  • RMA & Warranty Claims: Damaged unit returns to factory.
4. Finance, Reports & Admin (8 Modules)
  • GST Sales Invoicing: Automated CGST/SGST/IGST (HSN 8483).
  • Credit & Debit Notes: Rate variance adjustments & returns.
  • Accounts Receivable Ledger: Payment receipts & ageing tracker.
  • Order Profitability Analytics: Gross profit per transaction.
  • Global Search Engine: Search across serial number, item code, PO.
  • Automated PDF & Email Engine: 1-Click branded dispatch.
  • Role-Based Security (RBAC): Granular permissions by department.
  • Disaster Recovery: Automated 3-tier rolling database backups.

3. Zero Recurring Cloud Cost & On-Premise LAN Architecture

Unlike SAP or Odoo (which charge mandatory recurring subscriptions of ₹2,000 to ₹3,500/user/month), the OMPI ERP runs directly on your central office PC or laptop. Staff members access the system via Google Chrome or Edge over your local office Wi-Fi network (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:3000). Sensitive financial margins and supplier costs never leave your physical premises.
