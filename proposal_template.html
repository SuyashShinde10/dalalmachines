Comprehensive Breakdown of OMPI ERP Modules

The OMPI ERP system is structured across 4 specialized operational engines covering sales management, European import costing from OMPI Italy, warehouse spare parts tracking, on-site technician servicing, and GST accounting:

1. Sales & Customer Pipeline (10 Modules)
  • Customer Master: Billing/shipping branches, GSTIN, PAN.
  • Customer 360° View: Single-pane audit of orders, quotes, and dues.
  • Enquiry Pipeline: Status stages with mandatory lost analysis.
  • Costing Sheet: Landed cost, gross margin % & profit analytics.
  • Quotation Engine: Immutable revisions (V1, V2) with approvals.
  • Customer PO (CPO): PO validation, delivery terms & variance check.
  • Sales Order (SO): Confirmed orders linked to warehouse sourcing.
  • Proforma Invoice (PI): Configurable milestone splits (e.g. 30/50/20).
  • Communication Log: Time-stamped staff follow-up history.
  • Approval Workflow: Manager authorization for custom quotes.
2. Purchase & Italy Landed Cost (7 Modules)
  • Supplier Master: OMPI Italy & domestic tooling vendor profiles.
  • Supplier RFQs: Price & lead-time requests directly to Italy.
  • Supplier Quotations: Multi-vendor comparison & currency tracking.
  • Supplier PO (SPO): Advance, BL milestone & dispatch terms.
  • Multi-Currency FX Engine: EUR to INR live landed conversion.
  • Customs & Duty Calculator: Landed unit cost (Freight + Port).
  • Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Physical tally against packing list.
3. Warehouse, Spares & Retrofits (8 Modules)
  • SKU Spares Inventory: Clutch plates, discs, seals, and valves.
  • Bin Location Engine: Rack/bin coordinate mapping in Pune godown.
  • Stock Transfer: Inter-warehouse transfers between locations.
  • Delivery Challans (DC): Full and partial dispatch challans.
  • Gate Passes: Returnable & non-returnable security gate passes.
  • Digital Job Work Cards: On-site technician press clutch retrofits.
  • Quality Control (QC): Defect logging, tolerance and rework tracking.
  • RMA & Warranty Claims: Damaged unit returns to factory.
4. Finance, Reports & Admin (8 Modules)
  • GST Sales Invoicing: Automated CGST/SGST/IGST (HSN 8483).
  • Credit & Debit Notes: Rate variance adjustments & returns.
  • Accounts Receivable: Payment receipts & customer ageing tracker.
  • Profitability Analytics: Gross profit per transaction & part.
  • Global Search: Instant search across serial number, item code, PO.
  • Automated PDF Engine: 1-Click branded invoice & quote dispatch.
  • Role-Based Access (RBAC): Granular permissions by staff role.
  • Disaster Recovery: Automated 3-tier rolling database backups.

On-Premise LAN Architecture & Zero Recurring Cloud Cost

Commercial cloud ERP systems (SAP Business One, Odoo, NetSuite) enforce mandatory recurring subscriptions ranging from ₹2,000 to ₹3,500 per user/month (costing ₹3,00,000+ every single year). In contrast, the OMPI Central ERP is engineered for On-Premise Local Deployment. It runs securely on your designated central office PC/laptop. Sensitive financial margins, Italian supplier pricing, and customer records never touch third-party cloud servers.
