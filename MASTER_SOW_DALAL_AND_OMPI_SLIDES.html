Engineering Sizing Modules 08 to 10

  • Mod 08: Clutch Sizing Calculator: Engineer enters press tonnage (e.g. 630T), strokes per minute (SPM), and flywheel inertia. The system calculates required torque in Nm instantly.
  • Mod 09: Model Matching Engine: Automatically selects the perfect OMPI clutch-brake combination unit from the master catalog, matching exact mechanical press safety factors.

  • Mod 11: Bill of Materials (BOM): Automatically lists all required sub-components: friction discs, return springs, piston seals, and solenoid valves for the selected unit.

  • Mod 13: Retrofit Configuration Guide: Step-by-step shaft mounting and air-line pressure guidelines for replacing outdated clutches on older Indian and European presses.
