1.2 Standardized 10-Parameter Machinery Engineering Grid

Parameter Field Engineering Description & Schema Standard Buyer Committee Utility

3. Stroke Length & Adjustment Slide travel distance (mm) and motorized slide adjustment range. Die shut-height & tooling compatibility
4. Continuous Speed (SPM) Strokes per minute rating under continuous and single-stroke modes. Production cycle time & output throughput
5. Bed & Bolster Dimensions Left-to-Right × Front-to-Back platen dimensions (mm). Die footprint & bolster plate clearance
6. Die Shut Height (SDAU) Distance between bed and slide with stroke down, adjustment up. Existing customer tooling fitment verification
7. Clutch & Brake Mechanism Pneumatic friction, hydraulic, or mechanical unit (OMPI compatible). Safety compliance, cycle speed, and retrofit status
8. Main Drive Motor Power Electrical motor rating in kW / HP, voltage, and flywheel RPM. Factory power substation capacity requirement
9. Gross Physical Weight Total assembled machine weight in Metric Tons (approx. MT). Foundation civil design & transport logistics
10. Physical Condition & Trial Operational status: Under Power, Fully Tested, As-Inspected. Readiness for immediate live stockyard inspection

Minimum Host PC Hardware Specs Client Domain

  • Dedicated UPS & Power: Dedicated UPS with minimum 30-minute battery backup to prevent database write corruption during unexpected power outages.
  • Local Network Connectivity: Standard Gigabit Ethernet switch or Wi-Fi 6 router connecting staff laptops and office desktops.
  • Client Maintenance Scope: Hardware maintenance, electrical power supply, Windows/Linux OS licensing, and commercial antivirus protection remain client responsibilities.
Architecture, Concurrency & SecOps Engineering Baseline
  • Concurrency Baseline: High-performance ACID SQLite architecture optimized for up to 15–20 concurrent staff users across sales, warehouse, and accounts.
  • Cryptographic Salting: Bcrypt with 10 salt rounds (bcrypt.genSaltSync(10)) protecting all user passwords; zero plain-text credentials stored anywhere.
  • Adaptive Rate Limiting: Automated IP-based request throttling protecting authentication routes against brute-force attacks.
  • Defined 3-Tier Backups: Tier 1: Daily local SQLite snapshot; Tier 2: Daily synchronized mirror copy to external USB drive; Tier 3: Weekly encrypted off-site cloud archive.
