END-TO-END CAPABILITIES

6 PILLARS OF SEAMLESS HEAVY MACHINERY IMPORT

From locating rare presses abroad to on-site power testing, High Seas Sales in Indian Rupees, and doorstep port-to-pit delivery across India.

01

Ready European & Russian Stock

We buy and hold ready stock of heavy crank presses, knuckle joint coining machines, and horizontal boring tools in partner warehouses across Germany, Italy, Russia, and Poland for immediate dispatch.

  • ✓ Immediate dispatch without factory waiting times
  • ✓ Custom sourcing through 20-year international network
02

On-Site Power Inspection

We arrange physical inspection of presses at seller sites abroad under live electrical power. Customers can inspect slide alignment, clutch engagement, and hydraulic pressure before committing.

  • ✓ Power testing video recordings provided
  • ✓ Foundation drawings & original electrical manuals
03

High Seas Sales in INR

We deliver the presses on a High Seas Sales basis. Customers import directly in Indian Rupees, eliminating foreign exchange risk while obtaining the Bill of Entry in their name for full GST credit.

  • ✓ Zero foreign currency exposure or hedging cost
  • ✓ 100% claimable GST Input Tax Credit
04

Heavy CIF Maritime Shipping

We handle specialized break-bulk vessel chartering and ocean freight for extreme tonnage machines, having successfully delivered single press assemblies weighing up to 6,300 tons.

  • ✓ Competitive ocean freight agreements
  • ✓ Professional maritime heavy rigging & lashing
05

Port Customs Clearance

Our experienced customs clearing desk at Mumbai Port and Nhava Sheva (JNPT) manages documentation, customs valuation, inspection endorsements, and prompt clearance.

  • ✓ Zero demurrage expedited clearance
  • ✓ Complete compliance under Indian Customs Act
06

Doorstep Heavy Logistics

Turnkey transportation arranged on hydraulic multi-axle puller trailers directly to your factory foundation pit anywhere in India (Pune, Chennai, Bangalore, Ludhiana, Rajkot, Jamshedpur).
