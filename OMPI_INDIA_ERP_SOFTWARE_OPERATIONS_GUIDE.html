2.1 Engine 2: Engineering BOM & Italy Landed Cost

Engine 2 international import procurement, customs valuation, and mechanical engineering calculations ko govern karta hai:

Module Code & Name Core Technical Function Practical Business Use (Kaam)
Module 11
Multi-Level Bill of Materials (BOM)		 Hierarchical assembly BOM for combined clutch-brake units: Housing casting, friction discs, piston seals, springs, safety valve. Enables OMPI engineers to break down complete units into replaceable sub-components for service and refurbishment.
Module 12
Italy Import Purchase Order (PO)		 Automated PO generation in EUR formatted for OMPI S.r.l. (Italy). Includes OEM part numbers, delivery incoterms (Ex-Works / FCA). Direct standardized ordering to Italian factory without manual drafting; tracks outstanding international procurement.
Module 13
Landed Cost & Customs Calculator		 Algorithmic cost buildup: Ex-works EUR → Air/Sea freight → Assessable Value → BCD (7.5%) → SWS (10%) → IGST (18%) → CHA & inland transport. Calculates exact per-unit landed cost in INR down to the rupee, ensuring complete clarity on gross margins before quoting.
Module 14
Shipment & Bill of Lading (BL) Ledger		 International tracking: Airway Bill (AWB) / Sea BL numbers, shipping line, customs port arrival at Nhava Sheva / Mumbai Airport, clearance alerts. Provides real-time visibility into import pipeline so sales staff can give accurate delivery dates to Indian press customers.
Module 15
Technical Drawing & GA Repository		 Storage and instant lookup of General Arrangement (GA) drawings, CAD 2D/3D models, shaft fitment tolerances, keyway dimensions. Enables immediate sharing of dimensional drawings with customer machine designers for flywheel machining verification.
Module 16
Torque & Pneumatic Pressure Sizing		 Interactive formula engine calculating dynamic torque (Nm), thermal dissipation (kJ/min), and required air pressure (5.5 to 6.0 bar). Guarantees that selected clutch has sufficient safety margin (typically 1.5x to 2.0x of nominal press torque) to prevent slippage.
Module 17
Import Duty & HSN Tariff Master		 Statutory repository of customs tariff codes: HSN 84836090 (Clutches/shaft couplings), 84812000 (Safety valves) with statutory updates. Ensures customs documentation conforms to Indian Customs and ICEGATE classifications, avoiding port penalties.
Landed Cost Formula Architecture (Module 13 Calculation Standard)

Landed Cost = CIF Value (Ex-Works EUR × FX Rate + Freight + Ins) + Basic Customs Duty (BCD @ 7.5%) + Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS @ 10% of BCD) + Port Clearance / CHA Charges + Inland Transport to Bhosari Yard.

*IGST (18%) is paid at customs and tracked as Input Tax Credit (ITC) under Module 27 for complete cash flow optimization.

4.0 End-to-End User Journeys (Sales & Import Cycles)

Detailed walkthrough of daily business cycles across sales engineering and international procurement:

Workflow 1: Customer Clutch Sizing & Commercial Fast-Quote Flow

Scenario: Auto forge shop in Pune requires urgent clutch retrofit for a 1000T Mechanical Press:

1
Lead Intake & Sizing Calculation (Modules 01 & 06)
Sales engineer opens ERP, verifies customer GSTIN in Module 01, and inputs press specifications into Module 06 (Machine Compatibility Matrix). Sizing formula recommends OMPI Combined Clutch-Brake Unit (Rated Torque: 16,000 Nm @ 5.5 bar).
2
Landed Cost & Margin Verification (Modules 03 & 07)
System computes landed cost from Italy price list (EUR 8,200 × current FX + customs duty). Module 07 applies standard OEM discount matrix and verifies that gross profit margin is ≥22%.
3
Fast-Quote Generation & WhatsApp Dispatch (Modules 04 & 10)
Sales rep clicks "Generate Official Quotation". High-resolution PDF with OMPI corporate header, dimensional GA drawing, and payment milestones (30/50/20) is created and sent to the client's procurement head via WhatsApp.
4
Proforma Invoice & Sales Order Conversion (Modules 08 & 09)
Upon verbal agreement, system generates Proforma Invoice (PI). Once 30% mobilization advance is received, PI is locked into a Confirmed Sales Order (SO) with a single click.

Workflow 2: Italy Import Procurement & Landed Cost Valuation Flow

Scenario: Placing back-to-back OEM purchase order with OMPI S.r.l. Italy:

1
Triggering Import Purchase Order in EUR (Module 12)
Confirmed SO automatically prompts procurement desk to generate official Purchase Order to OMPI S.r.l. (Italy) in EUR, referencing exact factory part numbers and dispatch incoterms (FCA Italy).
2
Shipment Tracking & Customs Port Clearance (Module 14)
Goods depart Milan airport/Genoa port. Logistics manager logs Airway Bill / Bill of Lading (BL) into Module 14. System tracks expected arrival date at Mumbai customs and alerts Custom House Agent (CHA).
3
Customs Duty Assessment & Landed Valuation (Module 13)
Module 13 ingests customs Bill of Entry (BOE). Computes BCD (7.5%), SWS (10%), IGST (18%), and port charges, generating the definitive landed inventory valuation for the units.

4.3 Administrative Security & SecOps Architecture

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Users are assigned distinct roles: Admin (Managing Partner), Sales Engineer (Quotes & Catalog), Storekeeper (Inventory & DC), and Accountant (Invoicing & GST). Strict permissions prevent unauthorized changes.

Password Salting & Rate Limiting

All staff passwords are cryptographic salted hashes using Bcrypt with 10 salt rounds. Zero plaintext storage. API endpoints protected by express-rate-limit to prevent brute-force intrusion.

5.0 LAN Hardware Specs, Disaster Recovery & Sign-Off

On-premise infrastructure requirements, disaster recovery protocols, and formal client sign-off:

Minimum Recommended Server PC Hardware
  • Processor: Intel Core i5 / i7 (10th Gen or newer) or AMD Ryzen 5.
  • System Memory: 16 GB DDR4 RAM (ensures instant sub-5ms caching).
  • Storage Drive: 512 GB NVMe SSD (fast database read/write cycles).
  • Power Backup: 1 KVA dedicated Online UPS (prevents sudden power cuts).
  • Local Network: Gigabit Ethernet Switch (1000 Mbps) or Wi-Fi 6 router.
3-Tier Disaster Recovery Architecture
  • Tier 1 (Local): Automated daily rolling database snapshots on server SSD.
  • Tier 2 (External): Weekly encrypted export to an external USB hard drive.
  • Tier 3 (Offsite): Monthly encrypted backup stored at secondary safe location.
  • RTO / RPO Target: Recovery Time Objective under 30 minutes; RPO max 24 hours.
