Company Profile

TWO DECADES OF GLOBAL HEAVY MACHINERY IMPORT EXPERTISE

Established in Mumbai, Dalal Machine Tools Agency Pvt. Ltd. is recognized as one of India's preeminent importers and suppliers of heavy-duty used metal forming and metal cutting machinery.

Our core specialty centers on High-Tonnage Forging Presses (up to 6,300 Tons), Knuckle Joint Coining Presses, Mechanical C-Frame & Straight-Sided Stamping Presses, and precision CNC Horizontal Boring & Milling equipment.

Under the executive direction of Mr. Vinit Dalal (Managing Director), we bridge premier European, Russian, and Asian industrial sellers with Indian manufacturing conglomerates, forging shops, and automotive Tier-1 suppliers.

Global Network

International Machinery Sourcing Hubs

Our procurement network extends across premier industrial manufacturing nations, maintaining established broker and factory relationships in:

Russia & Ukraine: Heavy Voronezh (TMP) hot forging presses, 1000T-4000T knuckle joint coining lines.
Germany & Europe: Eumuco wedge presses, Kieserling stamping lines, DMG Mori & TOS CNC machinery.
Thailand & Vietnam: National & Komatsu hot forging presses, Enomoto screw presses, Fonyu mechanical lines.
Pune & Ludhiana Yards: Ready stock of C-Frame stamping presses (80T - 250T) and coining equipment.
Technical Partnership

OMPI India LLP • Pneumatic Clutch-Brake Systems

To provide complete turn-key commissioning, Dalal Machine Tools partners with OMPI India LLP. We supply and retrofit authentic Italian-designed pneumatic and hydraulic clutch-brake units for mechanical presses, ensuring maximum safety, rapid cycle rates, and ISO standard compliance.
