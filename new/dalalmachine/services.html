High Seas Sales: Legal, Customs & Tax FAQs

Tailored guidance for Chief Financial Officers (CFOs), Company Secretaries, and Plant Procurement Committees on zero forex exposure, high seas legal title transfer, and 100% eligible GST Input Tax Credit (ITC).

Consult Our CFO Desk

A High Seas Sale (HSS) is recognized under Section 2(4) of the Customs Act, 1962, and the Indian Sale of Goods Act, 1930. The title of the consignment is transferred by endorsement of the Bill of Lading (B/L) on international high seas before the vessel crosses the territorial customs waters of India. The original importer (Dalal Machine Tools) legally assigns all import rights and title to the buyer.

Under GST Circular No. 33/2017-Customs, no IGST is levied at the time of signing the High Seas Agreement. Instead, Integrated GST (IGST) is assessed and paid only once when filing the Bill of Entry at Nhava Sheva (JNPT) or Mumbai Port in the buyer's name. This IGST payment is directly uploaded to ICEGATE and reflected in your GSTR-2B, making it 100% eligible for immediate Input Tax Credit (ITC) offset against your domestic output liabilities.

The Bill of Entry is officially assessed and filed directly under the Ultimate Buyer's Import-Export Code (IEC) and GSTIN. Dalal Machine Tools acts as the high seas facilitator and manages all Customs Broker (CHA) documentation, port stevedoring, and gateway clearance.

When buying directly from European or Japanese sellers, Indian buyers face currency fluctuation between EUR/USD and INR during the 4 to 8 weeks of ocean transit. Under Dalal Machine Tools' High Seas contract, the total CIF price is fixed in Indian Rupees (INR). Dalal Machine Tools absorbs all hedging costs and foreign exchange volatility.

Your finance department receives a complete legal docket: (a) Notarized High Seas Sale Agreement on non-judicial stamp paper, (b) High Seas Commercial Invoice in INR, (c) Endorsed Original Bill of Lading, (d) Marine Cargo Insurance Policy certificate, and (e) Certified Chartered Engineer (CE) Inspection Report verifying machinery age, condition, and residual life.

Unlike direct overseas imports requiring 100% advance foreign TT or costly foreign documentary Letters of Credit (LC) with bank margin blocks and confirmation fees, High Seas transactions are settled via domestic Indian RTGS/NEFT against milestone inspection and dispatch dockets, preserving your company's working capital limits.

We negotiate 14 to 21 days of pre-approved container free-time directly with leading ocean carriers (Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd) before shipment. Our dedicated Mumbai Customs House Agent (CHA) team files prior Bill of Entry documentation ahead of vessel berthing, guaranteeing zero demurrage and expedited customs out-of-charge.
