Why Leading Indian Forgers Choose High Seas Sales

Importing heavy machinery directly from overseas exposes Indian manufacturers to currency volatility, LC complications, and customs clearance liabilities.

Through our High Seas Sales Agreement, Dalal Machine Tools transfers the vessel consignment title while on international waters. You pay in Indian Rupees (INR), avoid cross-border wire risks, and obtain the official Bill of Entry directly in your company's name for 100% GST input tax credit!

The 4-Step Turnkey Import & Commissioning Protocol

How Dalal Machine Tools delivers multi-thousand-ton forging presses and heavy boring lines from European factories directly to Indian foundation pits.
Step 01 • Sourcing & Frame Pre-Audit 01

Global Factory Sourcing & Ultrasonic Frame Integrity Audit

Personal inspection of heavy forging presses and CNC machinery across industrial corridors in Germany, Poland, Russia, and Thailand. Every candidate press is cross-referenced with original manufacturer serial records to certify zero frame cracks, unground crankshaft journals, and pristine bed casting integrity before any purchase commitment.

Germany, Poland, Russia, Thailand Desks 100% Crack-Free Casting Guarantee Original Foundation Blueprints Retrieved Chartered Engineer (CE) Customs Audit
Deliverable: Certified Technical Feasibility & Residual Lifespan Report 7 – 14 Days
Step 02 • Live Power Demonstration 02

Live Electrical Power Run & Micrometer Tolerance Trials Abroad

Before overseas disconnection, our technical directors run the press under live electrical power at the overseas factory. We cycle the flywheel, inspect pneumatic clutch-brake engagement timing, and record dial-indicator readings for bed-to-ram parallelism, gib clearances, and hydraulic decay tests with high-definition video documentation.

Live Continuous Power Cycling Bed-to-Ram Parallelism Tested (≤0.05mm) 4K Video Protocol for Plant Engineers Supervised Overseas Client Witness Trips
Deliverable: 4K Video Inspection Protocol & Precision Geometric Log 3 – 5 Days
Step 03 • High Seas Title Transfer 03

High Seas Sales Agreement Transfer in Indian Rupees (INR)

Under Section 2(4) of the Customs Act 1962, title is transferred on international waters before customs entry. You pay Dalal Machine Tools strictly in Indian Rupees (INR), completely eliminating foreign currency exchange fluctuations. The customs Bill of Entry is assessed directly in your company name, ensuring 100% full eligibility for GST Input Tax Credit (ITC).

100% Pure INR Settlement (Zero Forex Risk) Section 2(4) Customs Act 1962 Compliant Direct 100% GST ITC Input Tax Credit LC or Staged Milestone Commercial Terms
Deliverable: Notarized High Seas Sale Agreement & Direct Bill of Entry On Vessel Sailing
Step 04 • Doorstep Rigging & Handover 04

Nhava Sheva Port Stevedoring, Multi-Axle Convoy & Pit Placement

Turnkey logistics from heavy-lift breakbulk vessel offloading at Nhava Sheva or Mumbai Port directly onto your factory pad. Dalal coordinates port stevedoring, customs duty appraisal, en-route state transit permissions, and specialized multi-axle hydraulic puller trailers (Goldhofer / Tratec) delivering multi-hundred-ton machinery directly into your foundation pit.
